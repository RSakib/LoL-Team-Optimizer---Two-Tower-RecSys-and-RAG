# League artwork

Champion portraits are Riot Games Data Dragon assets: 165 copied without
modification from the supplied 13.22.1 archive; six newer champions downloaded
individually from patch 15.14.1 (the dataset's patch 25.14). `catalog.json` records
the source of every portrait. Only champions in the real processed profiles are
selectable; artwork is not player or recommendation data.

Rank crests are copied without modification from Riot's
[ranked-emblems-latest.zip](https://static.developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol/ranked-emblems-latest.zip),
linked in [Riot's developer documentation](https://support-developer.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/22698698001939-League-of-Legends#icons-and-emblems).
Downloaded September 16, 2026. Only the six supported rank crests are bundled.

The four division SVGs are original, simple Roman-numeral UI badges, **not official
Riot division artwork**. The supplied archive has no standalone division badges;
Riot's legacy tier-icons archive combines obsolete tier art with divisions and
does not include Emerald. These neutral badges do not suggest a different tier.

League of Legends and its artwork belong to Riot Games. This is an unofficial
fan-made portfolio project, not endorsed or sponsored by Riot Games.
